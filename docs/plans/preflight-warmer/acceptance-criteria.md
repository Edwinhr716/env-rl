## Scope

The pre-flight puts task images on the nodes of the placement domains before a run, so that a first
boot does not pull and unpack the image. This file covers:

- `ListPlacementDomains` and `WarmImage` of `ate-env-api`
  ([internal/apiservice/placement.go](../../../internal/apiservice/placement.go)) and their
  `ate-env` commands;
- `ate-env manifest --domains`, `--node-selector` and `ate-env manifest workerpool`
  ([cmd/ate-env/manifest.go](../../../cmd/ate-env/manifest.go));
- the planner and the warmer of `swebench-loader`
  ([internal/benchmarking/swebench/preflight_plan.go](../../../internal/benchmarking/swebench/preflight_plan.go),
  [preflight.go](../../../internal/benchmarking/swebench/preflight.go)), the `run` flags
  `-placement` and `-preflight`, and the evidence Validity Rule 1 reads;
- `ate_env_rl.PreflightWarmer`
  ([clients/python/src/ate_env_rl/preflight.py](../../../clients/python/src/ate_env_rl/preflight.py))
  and `FleetConfig(preflight=True)`.

Out of scope: `SandboxFleet.preflight_report()` (`FL-1` to `FL-4` of
[fleet-sdk](../fleet-sdk/acceptance-criteria.md)), Harbor's warm step as a command (`HB-23` of
[harbor](../harbor/acceptance-criteria.md)), and the derived templates and their goldens (`AE-*` of
[ate-env-api](../ate-env-api/acceptance-criteria.md)).

Rows 11, 12 and 20 of the plan (the round strategy of `WarmImage`, the periodic sweep, the
projections and the node cache check) have no criteria yet, because their design is open. Each gets
its rows here before its code.

Terms: a probe is an environment that a warm call creates to make a node pull an image, and then
deletes. A pair is one image in one placement domain. The coverage of a pass is complete when each
pair reached the node count of its domain, each golden snapshot is ready (or the base template has
goldens disabled), and the pass was not cancelled. The coverage of one `WarmImage` call is complete
when the call reached its `wanted` nodes; it has no golden term.
